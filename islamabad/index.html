What we're about

We are a group of Amazon Web Services (AWS) enthusiasts, professionals,and students, in Islamabad who are passionate about sharing their knowledge, and learning about technology. We meet every few weeks to talk about the Tech Ecosystem, Cloud and how we build amazing products and solutions.
