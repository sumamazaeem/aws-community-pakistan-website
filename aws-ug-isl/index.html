AWS User Group Islamabad

We are a group of Amazon Web Services (AWS) enthusiasts, professionals,and students, in Islamabad who are passionate about sharing their knowledge, and learning about technology. We meet every few weeks to talk about the Tech Ecosystem, Cloud and how we build amazing products and solutions. We gather people working with lots of different technologies that take advantage of cloud solutions.

What we're about

Welcome to the AWS User Group Islamabad, a meetup group for the AWS user community. Proposed topics will cover the technical aspects of AWS feature use and implementation. We will tend to focus real-world use cases - what has worked and what hasn't. We are advocates for greater diversity & inclusion in Islamabad Tech and are especially keen to receive talk submissions from people from underrepresented groups.
