Rules & Regulations

Kirothon 2026 is a virtual hackathon open to all participants from Pakistan. Anyone from Pakistan can register and attend regardless of their city or location. Participants must be at least 18 years of age or have parental/guardian consent. Teams may consist of 1–4 members. Members of the AWS Community Pakistan organizing team and judges are not eligible to participate.
All submissions must be original work created during the hackathon period. Pre-existing projects are not eligible.
Participants retain ownership of their work. By submitting, participants grant AWS Community Pakistan the right to showcase their project.
All participants must adhere to the AWS Community Pakistan Code of Conduct. Harassment, discrimination, or disrespectful behavior will result in disqualification.
Projects that violate the rules, contain plagiarized code, or fail to meet submission requirements will be disqualified.
