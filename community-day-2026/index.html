Who Are We?

AWS Community Pakistan is a Pakistan-based group united by community-led learnings and diversified by tools and technologies closely knit with Amazon Web Services, cloud computing architecture, High-Performance Computing (HPC), DevOps, Artificial Intelligence, and production use cases.

AWS Community Days are community-organized cloud education events featuring technical discussions and live demos led by expert AWS users and industry leaders.

All skill levels are welcome to join our motto of learning, sharing, and networking. Let's empower the cloud community together!
