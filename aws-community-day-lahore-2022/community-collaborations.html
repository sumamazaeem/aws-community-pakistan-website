It is a great honor to convey that AWS Community Pakistan/ AWS User Group Lahore is organizing AWS Community Day Lahore 2022. This event will consist of technical discussions, workshops, and hands-on labs led by expert AWS users and industry leaders from around the world. This event will provide an elemental chance for intrigued cloud enthusiasts to polish their skills and enhance their learning.

For this purpose, AWS Community Pakistan/ AWS User Group Lahore would like to collaborate with your society so that the maximum number of students can benefit from this opportunity. In acknowledgment of your collaboration:

1) The official logo of your society will be printed alongside AWS Community Pakistan on a dedicated poster that is to be posted on our social media platforms. This will prove to be great for the branding of your society.
2) You will be awarded an official letter and certificate of collaboration.
3) You will be required to share promotional posts of AWS Community Day on your official page and other social media networks.
4) You will be required to send students from your society to attend the event.
5) You as well as your students coming from your reference will be eligible for AWS Credits worth 500 USD.
6) You as well as your students coming from your reference will be eligible for AWS Swags and giveaway items.
Looking forward to an awesome Collaboration and hearing from you.
